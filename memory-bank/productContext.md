# Product Context: AUUS (FAOS v6)

## Tai sao du an nay ton tai?
Quan ly quang cao COD cross-border rat phuc tap: nhieu ad accounts, nhieu thi truong, budget thay doi lien tuc. Can AI tu dong phan tich va hanh dong thay con nguoi.

## Van de giai quyet
1. Khong du thoi gian review tat ca campaigns moi ngay
2. Quyet dinh budget/bid cham, bo lo co hoi hoac mat tien
3. Thieu audit trail — khong biet tai sao AI/nguoi da lam quyet dinh X
4. Du lieu nam rai rac (BigQuery, Meta API, Sheets) — khong co cai nhin tong the

## User Experience Goals
- Real-time Live Feed: Xem AI dang lam gi ngay luc nay (SSE stream)
- Audit History: Tra cuu moi quyet dinh AI da lam + ly do
- Memory Viewer: Xem knowledge graph cua AI (FalkorDB)
- Approval Flow: Telegram/Discord buttons cho quyet dinh rui ro cao
- One-click Settings: Thay doi risk level, ROAS targets tu dashboard

## Doi thu / San pham tuong tu
- Revealbot: Tu dong hoa ads nhung khong co COD logic
- Madgicx: AI ads nhung khong support multi-currency cross-border
- FAOS: Custom-built, hieu COD, cross-border, multi-project
