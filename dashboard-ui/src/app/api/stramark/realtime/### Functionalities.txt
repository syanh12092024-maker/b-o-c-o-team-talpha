### Functionalities
By using API integration with euShipments.com, you can significantly enhance your order fulfillment process, leading to improved customer satisfaction and operational efficiency.
API integration between us and your e-shop enables a range of functionalities to optimize your online sales and business analytics:
Reporting on the stock quantity of your products in each fulfillment location
Information about active statuses of the orders
Tracking information and detailed history of each air waybill
Data and statistics about payments
Product, bundles and shipments generation
Air waybills (AWB) creation
Fulfillment order creation
Full Cash-on-delivery reports and analytics
